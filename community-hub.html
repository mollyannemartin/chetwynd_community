Community Hub

The Chetwynd Community Hub is at the heart of community life on Chetwynd Barracks. It provides a range of facilities, activities and support for Service personnel and their families.

The Hub

The Community Centre is located within The Hub, Building 17. The Hub is also home to the Chetwynd Barracks Welfare Team, Army Welfare Service Community Development Worker, Community Room, multi-faith area and Youth Centre.

Community Centre

The Community Room is the focus of many community events and activities at Chetwynd.

Coffee Mornings & Afternoons

Come along for an opportunity to meet other members of the community.

Stay & Play

Family-friendly sessions providing opportunities for children and families to get involved.

Community Library

The Community Room also hosts a community library.

Community Committee

The Chetwynd Community Committee gives members of the community an opportunity to have their say on what happens at Chetwynd.

The committee helps plan and deliver activities and events for the whole community, including family fun days, quiz nights, crafting sessions and more.

Want to get involved?

Meetings are held every other month on Wednesday evenings, 1900–2100, at the Welfare Hub.

Nibbles are provided and you are welcome to bring along a bottle.

Community activities, clubs and events rely on volunteers from the community.

If you can spare some time and would like to help, or would like to find out more about volunteering, contact Siobhan.
