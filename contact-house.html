Contact house - 40 Williams Road

40 williams can sleep up to 4 people, 1 double room and 1 twin room, and if needed travel cots can be provided. It can be booked for visiting family or friends at a cost of £30 per night. There is a minimum stay of 2 nights. It is fully furnished, you will just need to provide your own towels, toiletries, food, and drink.

Contact house - 38 Williams Road

38 williams can sleep up to 4 people, 1 double room and 1 twin room, and if needed travel cots can be provided. It can be booked for visiting family or friends at a cost of £30 per night and an additional £15 per night if bringing pets. There is a minimum stay of 2 nights. It is fully furnished, you will just need to provide your own towels, toiletries, food, and drink.

Welfare Facility / Soft Play

The Welfare Department building is available to book by service personnel for meetings, welfare support, and community gatherings. To book the facility a small fee is payable to the Welfare Fund. The Facility cost £10 per day/night or £50 for a weekend booking. A Cash deposit of £100 is required for booking outside of working hours, T&Cs apply.
