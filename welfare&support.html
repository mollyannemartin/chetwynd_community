Frequently Asked Questions

  1. How do I book an appointment?

    Just email the welfare JNCO and they can book you into the relevant person.

  2. Is the welfare office confidential?

    Yes, you can come in and close the door. Just let us know.

Still not sure who to contact?

If you're unsure where to start, contact the welfare team and we'll help direct you to the most appropriate service.
