Explore the Local Area

Whether you're newly posted to Chetwynd or just looking for somewhere new to explore, this page highlights useful local services, attractions and everyday essentials.

Getting Around

Chetwynd Barracks is approximately 7 miles from Nottingham city centre and 3 miles from Junction 25 of the M1. Long Eaton and Beeston are both approximately 2 miles from the Barracks, in opposite directions.

By Car

The main Guardroom postcode is NG9 5HA. Chetwynd Barracks can be accessed from Junction 25 of the M1 via the A52.

By Train

Attenborough railway station is the nearest station. Trains can be accessed via Nottingham or Derby. For residents with swipe-card access to the East Gate, the station is approximately a 20-minute walk from the Barracks. Access via the main gate is approximately 30 minutes on foot, 5 minutes by taxi or 15 minutes by public transport.

National Rail Enquiries

By Tram

Toton Lane tram station is located to the north of Chetwynd Barracks and can be accessed via the back gate. A key card is required to use this entrance. Without a key card, the main camp entrance is approximately a 20-minute walk away.

Taxis

  • Cable Cars: 0115 922 9229
  • Attenborough Taxi & Airport Service: 0115 917 0435
  • Uber – available via the Uber app.

Local Towns

Long Eaton

Approximately 2 miles from Chetwynd Barracks, Long Eaton offers a range of local shops, banks and building societies, as well as supermarkets and other everyday services.

Beeston

Approximately 2 miles from Chetwynd Barracks, Beeston offers a range of local shops, banks and building societies, as well as supermarkets, pharmacies and other everyday services.

Nottingham

Nottingham city centre is approximately 7 miles from Chetwynd Barracks and offers a wider range of shopping, entertainment, restaurants and attractions.

East Midlands Airport

East Midlands Airport is approximately a 20-minute drive from Chetwynd Barracks. A local Skylink bus service is available from McDonald's.
