Booking Information

House bookings are subject to availability and are not confirmed until you have received confirmation from the Welfare Office.

Arrival and Departure

Guests must arrive and depart at the agreed times. Any changes to your booking should be communicated to the Welfare Office as soon as possible.

Guests

Only the guests listed on the booking request may stay at the property. Please ensure all guest details are provided accurately.

Pets and Allergies

Pets must be declared on the booking request. Guests are responsible for informing the Welfare Office of any allergies or other relevant requirements before their stay.

Property and Cleaning

The property must be left clean and tidy before departure. All bedding must be removed and returned to the designated location.

Damage

Any damage to the property or its contents must be reported to the Welfare Office as soon as possible.

Cancellation

If you need to cancel your booking, please notify the Welfare Office as soon as possible so that the accommodation can be made available to others.
