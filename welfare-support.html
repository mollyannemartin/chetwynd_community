Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact the welfare team?

You can contact a member of the welfare team using the contact details provided on this page.

Is welfare support confidential?

Welfare conversations are treated with respect and confidentiality. If you're unsure about confidentiality, please speak to a member of the welfare team.

Who can use the welfare service?

Welfare support is available to all Service personnel and their families.

I'm not sure who can help me. What should I do?

That's okay. Contact the welfare team and we'll help you find the most appropriate person or organisation.
